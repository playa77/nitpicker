# WP-14 — Audio capture engine

Date: 2026-11-08. Baseline: origin/main `1681dc2` (fetched and fast-forwarded before implementation).

Added a dedicated IO AudioRecord loop at 16 kHz mono/PCM16 with complete 320-sample frames, real Chunker delivery, fatal microphone errors, orderly stop/drain and release. Session recovery resumes sequence numbers and offsets instead of colliding with prior chunk rows. The configured silence threshold is forwarded to Chunker.

[ASSUMPTION] stop is suspend: it stops the recorder, joins its read coroutine, and delivers the final buffered chunk before returning. This makes stop→drain ordering explicit without runBlocking. release provides non-suspending destruction cleanup.
